Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What we store

  • Your email address and password, used only to sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by Supabase, never in readable form, and we can't see them.
  • Your roadmap progress: which tasks and checkpoints you've completed, your task notes, your scratchpad, and your session log.

Where it's kept

In a Supabase database. Access rules mean only your signed-in account can read or change your progress. A copy also stays in your browser so the app works offline.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking scripts, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the menu (⋯) and choose Delete my account. That removes your account and everything saved in it right away. Signing out removes the account's copy from that device's sync bookkeeping; your progress stays in your account.
